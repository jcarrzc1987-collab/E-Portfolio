How old do I need to be to rent?

Drivers must be at least 21 for Corvettes and everyday cars, and at least 25 for exotics.

What do I need to bring?

A valid driver's license, a major credit card in the renter's name, and proof of full-coverage auto insurance.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes. A refundable hold of $500 for Corvettes and everyday cars and $2,500 for exotics is placed on your card and released after the car is returned.

Are there mileage limits?

Each rental includes 150 miles per day. Extra miles are $1 per mile. Planning a road trip? Tell us when you book.

What about fuel?

Return the car with the same fuel level you received it with. Premium fuel is required for Corvettes and exotics.

Do I need my own insurance?

Yes, your personal auto policy must cover rental cars. You can also add one of our optional protection plans for extra coverage.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup. To change dates, call or text us and we'll do our best to help.

How do I find you?

We're at 1849 W Drake Dr, Suite 102, in Tempe. Look for the big Indoor Autosales sign on the building. We're inside. Park out front and come in to our lounge. Open 24 hours. Get directions

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across the Phoenix metro area, including hotels and homes. Delivery fees depend on distance, and we'll confirm yours when you book.

Can I smoke in the car or bring pets?

No smoking or vaping in any vehicle. Pets are not allowed. A cleaning fee applies if either rule is broken.
